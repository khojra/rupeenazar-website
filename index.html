Questions

Good to know

How does RupeeNazar find my payments?

With Notification access on, RupeeNazar sees only notifications: the ones from your payment apps, and the one your phone shows when a bank SMS arrives (you can switch SMS off). It picks out the amount and name and sorts them. It has no SMS permission, never opens your inbox, and ignores every other notification.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. It stays on your phone. RupeeNazar has no internet permission. Backups go only where you choose, locked with your password.

Which banks and apps work?

Payment messages from major Indian banks, card issuers and UPI apps. Banks word their messages differently, so if one is missed or read wrongly, tell us and we'll teach the app.

A payment is in the wrong category. What do I do?

Tap it and pick the right one. RupeeNazar remembers your choice for that shop next time.

How do I add cash spends?

Tap + then Speak, and say several at once, like "chai 20, auto 60, kirana 340". Or type them in. ATM withdrawals show as cash in hand until you add what you spent it on.

How do I move to a new phone?

Save a backup on the old phone (or turn on the daily automatic backup). On the new phone, install RupeeNazar, open Backup and restore the file.

Is it financial advice?

No. RupeeNazar shows information about your own spending. For big money decisions, please talk to a qualified adviser or your CA.
