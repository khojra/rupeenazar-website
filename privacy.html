Privacy policy

In short: your money data stays on your phone. We can't see it, and we never sell it.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

RupeeNazar is an Android app made by the RupeeNazar team in Hyderabad, India. For anything about your privacy, write to support@rupeenazar.com.

2. What the app reads, and why

With your permission (Notification access), RupeeNazar reads notifications from payment apps, and bank SMS alerts if you switch that on, only to find payments: the amount, who was paid or who paid you, the account or card's last digits, and the date. It reads them as they arrive; it has no SMS permission and never opens your SMS inbox. Other notifications are ignored. One-time passwords (OTPs) are never saved.

3. Where your data is kept

Only on your phone, in RupeeNazar's private storage. RupeeNazar has no servers for your data and does not send your payments, messages, names or balances to us or to anyone else. The app does not have permission to use the internet. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

4. Backups

Only if you turn them on. A backup is locked with your own password before it leaves the phone, and saved where you choose, such as your own Google Drive. We can't open it and can't recover a forgotten password.

5. Files and feedback you send

When you download or share a month or a list of payments as a PDF or Excel sheet, the file is made on your phone and goes only where you send it. If you add a password to a PDF, RupeeNazar doesn't keep it.

If you send feedback or report a payment, your email app opens with exactly what will be sent: your words, a usage summary if you keep it (counts, plus which apps' notifications were checked and what happened; no amounts, names or message words), and any bank message you chose to include, with the balance and long numbers hidden. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

6. Voice entry

When you speak a spend, your phone's own speech service (usually Google's) turns your voice into text. If the language is downloaded on your phone, this happens on the phone; otherwise that service may process the audio. RupeeNazar only receives the text.

7. Investing and other apps

"Invest" only copies an amount and opens the investment app you chose. RupeeNazar never moves money and never sees your investment account.

8. Your choices

You can see and correct every payment in the app, export your data with a backup, and erase everything with Profile › Delete all my data. Uninstalling RupeeNazar also removes all its data from the phone.

9. Children

RupeeNazar is meant for adults (18 and over).

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and in the app, with the new date.

11. Questions and complaints

Write to the RupeeNazar team at support@rupeenazar.com. We'll reply as soon as we can.
